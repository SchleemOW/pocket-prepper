Privacy Policy

Pocket Prepper  ·  Last updated: May 2026

Pocket Prepper is an offline survival reference app. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data we do not collect

Pocket Prepper does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, location, contacts)
  • Chat history or questions you ask the app
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to external servers
  • Any data sent to Anthropic or any third-party service

How the app works

All processing happens entirely on your device. The AI model and knowledge databases are downloaded and stored locally. No internet connection is required after the initial module download. Nothing you type or ask is ever sent off your device.

Module downloads

When you download a knowledge module, the app makes a network request to GitHub to fetch the database file. No personal data is sent during this request — it is a standard HTTPS file download. GitHub's own privacy policy governs that connection.

On-device storage

Downloaded modules are stored in your device's local storage. You can delete them at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Children

Pocket Prepper does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and note the changes here.
